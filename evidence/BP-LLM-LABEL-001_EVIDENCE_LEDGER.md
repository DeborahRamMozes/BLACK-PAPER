## 8. Publication Discipline

The case proves repeated failure within one sustained professional workflow. It does not by itself establish population-wide failure rates. Broader claims must be supported by reproducible testing across models, plans, platforms, and users.

The Black Paper may be brutal. It must not be sloppy. Otherwise it becomes the same product foolishness it is dissecting.
